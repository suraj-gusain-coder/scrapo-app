Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SCRAPO doorstep service

Is there any minimum pickup quantity or weight?

For residential doorstep pickups, we typically recommend a minimum estimated weight of around 10-15 kg (or at least 1 large appliance / e-waste device) to make the pickup logistically viable. There is no maximum limit!

How is the scrap weighed?

Every verified SCRAPO agent is equipped with a calibrated, certified digital scale. The scrap is weighed right before your eyes, category by category, and logged directly into the agent's app.

How do I receive the money?

As soon as the weighing is complete, the agent triggers an instant UPI transfer to your UPI ID (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or bank transfer). A digital receipt is generated instantly on your phone with the exact weight breakdown.

How are collection agents verified?

All SCRAPO agents undergo background checks, Aadhaar verification, and formal training in polite customer service and digital scale handling. They wear official ID badges and you can track their live GPS location while they travel to your address.
